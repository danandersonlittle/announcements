Tub for Grub

Please fill the red Tub for Grub in the Connection Center with pancake mix that only needs water and syrup. Donations go to Good Samaritans of Garland for distribution to families in need.

Cans for Cows

On the last Sunday of the month, we collect aluminum cans to sell for scrap. Proceeds support Heifer International’s work to provide a cow for a family in need. In the past 41 years, the church has purchased 42 cows.
