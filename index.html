You have a place here.

Welcome to First Presbyterian Church of Garland! We are people gathered from diverse backgrounds and cultures, worshiping one Lord in different styles and languages.

Ways to help

Hope Clinic · Quarterly offering

This quarter’s Deacons’ Offering supports Hope Clinic of Garland, Inc., which provides medical care and resources for healthy living to underserved people in Dallas County. Its holistic approach addresses physical, emotional, and spiritual health, sharing the love of Jesus Christ through community partnership.

Give online

Tub for Grub

Bring pancake mix that only needs water and syrup to the red tub in the Connection Center. Donations go to Good Samaritans of Garland to help families in need.

Cans for Cows

On the last Sunday of the month, bring aluminum cans to be sold for scrap. Proceeds go to Heifer International to purchase a cow for a family in need. At the time of this bulletin, the congregation had purchased 42 cows in 41 years.
